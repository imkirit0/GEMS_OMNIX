**Nature of the document.** REFS is a **proposal for the target system**: it says "we are planning to build", it is a feature list, and it has no screens or rules. SPEC and WF describe the **legacy system as observed**. The two answer different questions, so "matches" below means "a matrix row covers this capability". It does not mean the behaviour has been verified.

**Evidence class.** REFS is 15 months older than SPEC and carries no sign-off, so every REFS item enters as **PMC** (owner: the business owner of the module) until the user confirms that REFS is approved scope (Q-REFS-1 below). Nothing in REFS overrides an SRC fact about legacy behaviour.

**Method.**
- Read all 17 pages in full (`pdftotext -layout`).
- Compared each REFS item against the 290-row matrix (`data/matrix-*.tsv`) and the decision register (04).
- Grepped SPEC/WF text for every term that had no matrix hit, so a "NEW" item is also absent from SPEC/WF.
- Status codes:
  - **MATCH**: a matrix row covers it.
  - **PARTIAL**: covered, but REFS adds something.
  - **NEW**: no row, and not in SPEC/WF.
  - **CONFLICT**: REFS contradicts legacy behaviour or an audit design decision.

## B. Modules with no coverage at all
1. **Mobile apps** (#69, #70, #114). They add a student identity and login, online payment by students, certificate download, and a native or PWA client. This is a new stage in the roadmap (10), and it adds T-AUTH scope (student auth differs from staff ADR-001).
2. **External exam integrations** (#74, #75, #79): the Exam Portal API, the Gobsbank API, and result fetch. The counterpart systems, contracts and owners are unknown.
3. **Accounts extensions** (#91, #95–97): bill-by-bill, Schedule III, expense workflow. These are G-FIN items (07).
